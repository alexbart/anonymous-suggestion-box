# Nurse User Guide

## Anonymous Suggestion Box

Welcome. This guide explains how to submit an anonymous suggestion
and how to check its status later.

The system is intentionally simple. It is designed for hospital
nursing staff who may only have a few minutes between shifts.

---

## 1. What this system does

- Lets you submit a written suggestion from a phone or computer.
- Generates a reference number you can use to check the status later.
- Stores your suggestion safely. Management sees only what you wrote,
  not who wrote it.

## 2. What this system does NOT do

- It does not require your name, email, phone number, staff ID,
  ward, or shift.
- It does not reveal your identity to anyone.
- It does not create an account for you.
- It does not log in to your phone or your browser profile.

> The only identifier your suggestion carries is a randomly generated
> reference number such as `SB-B9FKMU`. Hold on to it if you want to
> follow up.

## 3. How to submit a suggestion

### On your phone

1. Open the URL your hospital has given you for the Suggestion Box.
   The page should look like this:

   ```
   ┌──────────────────────────────┐
   │                              │
   │  💬  Anonymous Suggestion     │
   │      Box                     │
   │                              │
   │  Tell us what is happening,  │
   │  what could be improved, or  │
   │  what needs attention.       │
   │                              │
   │  Your identity is not         │
   │  required.                   │
   │  ────────────────────────    │
   │  Please do not include        │
   │  patient names, numbers,      │
   │  phone numbers, or other      │
   │  identifying information.     │
   │                              │
   └──────────────────────────────┘
   ```

2. **Choose a category.** Tap the dropdown under
   "What is your suggestion about?" and pick the option that fits best.

   | Option            | Example use                          |
   |-------------------|---------------------------------------|
   | Patient Care      | Quality of care, patient experience   |
   | Staffing          | Workload, shift patterns, ratios      |
   | Equipment         | Broken devices, missing tools         |
   | Workplace Safety  | Unsafe environment, hazards           |
   | Staff Welfare     | Burnout, rest areas, support          |
   | Management        | Decisions, communication from above  |
   | Communication     | Handoffs, team information flow       |
   | Other             | Anything that does not fit the above  |

3. **Choose how important it is.** Tap the option that best matches
   the urgency of the issue.

   - **Low** — small improvement, no rush
   - **Normal** — worth addressing when possible
   - **Important** — needs attention soon
   - **Urgent** — patient or staff safety may be at risk

   If you do not pick, it defaults to **Normal**.

4. **Write your suggestion.** Tap inside the box under
   "Your suggestion" and describe what is happening.
   Try to include:
   - What happened
   - Where it happened
   - When it happened
   - What you think could be improved

   Aim for at least 10 characters. The box holds up to 5000.

   > Avoid patient names, patient numbers, phone numbers, or other
   > identifying information.

5. **Attach supporting files (optional).** You can attach up to 5
   images or documents, for example a photograph of a broken device
   or a ward checklist.

   Accepted file types:

   - JPG / JPEG
   - PNG
   - WebP
   - PDF
   - DOCX (Microsoft Word)

   File size limits:

   - Up to 10 MB per file
   - Up to 25 MB in total

6. **Submit.** Tap the dark "Submit anonymously" button. While
   the suggestion is being sent, the button reads "Submitting…"
   and cannot be tapped again.

### On a computer

The steps are the same. The screen simply has more room.

---

## 4. After you submit

When the submission succeeds, the page changes to:

```
   ✓

   Suggestion submitted

   Thank you for sharing your suggestion.
   Your submission has been received anonymously.

   YOUR REFERENCE NUMBER

   SB-B9FKMU

   Save this reference number. You can use it later
   to check the status of your suggestion.
```

You will see a reference number that looks like `SB-XXXXXX`.
**Write it down or take a photo of the screen** if you want to
check the status later.

You will also see two buttons:

- **Check my submission** — jumps to the status page for this
  reference immediately.
- **Submit another suggestion** — clears the form so you can write
  a second one.

---

## 5. How to check the status later

1. Open the Suggestion Box URL.
2. Scroll to the bottom of the page.
3. Tap **"Already submitted? Check your status"**.
4. On the next screen, type your reference number
   (e.g. `SB-B9FKMU`) and tap **"Check status"**.

You will see something like:

```
   Reference number
   SB-B9FKMU

   Current status
   [ New ]

   Your suggestion has been received and is waiting
   for review.
```

### What the status values mean

| Status         | What it means                                              |
|----------------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| New            | Received and waiting for management to review              |
| Under Review   | Management is actively looking at it                       |
| Pending        | Action is being planned, but not yet started               |
| Actioned       | Action has been taken or a decision has been made          |
| Closed         | The matter has been resolved or the file is closed         |

The status can go **backwards** (for example, Under Review →
Pending → Under Review) if management needs more time.

### If you see "We couldn't find a submission with that reference number"

This means one of the following:

- You typed the code incorrectly. Try again, paying attention to
  the case (it must be uppercase) and the dash.
- You submitted from a different Suggestion Box than the one you
  are checking.
- The code is more than 6 characters after the dash.

### If you see "Please enter a valid reference number"

The format is `SB-` followed by 6 uppercase letters or numbers.
For example, `SB-B9FKMU` or `SB-2ZT8H5`.

---

## 6. What to write (and what not to write)

### Do write

- Specific situations
- Patterns you have noticed
- Suggestions for improvement
- Safety concerns
- Equipment problems

### Do not write

- Patient names
- Patient numbers (MRN / hospital ID / NHS number)
- Phone numbers
- Colleague names, if you can avoid it
- Your own name, ward, or shift

The system will store whatever you type. The hospital takes the
anonymity promise seriously, but please help by avoiding
identifying details.

---

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**Will anyone know I submitted this?**
No. The system does not record your name, email, phone, staff ID,
ward, or shift. The only way to identify a suggestion is by its
random reference number, which only you see.

**What if I lose my reference number?**
Unfortunately, without the reference number there is no way to look
up your suggestion later. Take a photo of the success screen as
soon as you see it.

**Can I edit my suggestion after submitting?**
No. If you need to add information, submit a new suggestion that
references the original (you can include the reference number in
the message).

**How long until I see a status change?**
That depends on your hospital's process. The system itself
updates immediately when management makes a change.

**Is this an emergency channel?**
No. If something is urgent and needs immediate attention, use
your normal escalation path (e.g. in-charge nurse, safety officer,
hospital emergency line). The Suggestion Box is for non-urgent
feedback that still deserves to be heard.

**Who decides what to do with my suggestion?**
Hospital management. The Suggestion Box is the channel; the
decisions are made by the people in your hospital who review the
submissions.

**Can I see other people's suggestions?**
No. Only the people who submit a suggestion, and only via the
reference number, can look at it. Management sees all
suggestions; nurses do not.

## 8. Troubleshooting

| Problem                                | What to do                                   |
|----------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Page will not load                     | Try a different network or another device    |
| "Please enter a valid reference number"| Make sure the code is `SB-` + 6 chars         |
| "We couldn't find a submission..."     | Double-check the code; case must be uppercase|
| File upload says "larger than 10 MB"   | Compress or split the file                   |
| File upload says "not supported"      | Use JPG, PNG, WebP, PDF, or DOCX            |
| Button says "Submitting…" but nothing  | Wait a few seconds, then refresh the page    |
| You submitted by mistake               | Submit a follow-up clarifying the situation |

For technical issues with the website, contact the person who
shared the URL with you.

---

## 9. Privacy promise

This system was designed with one purpose: to let you speak up
without risk.

What the system stores:

- What you wrote
- Optional attachments
- A random reference code
- The category, priority, and status

What the system does **not** store:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your phone number
- Your staff ID
- Your ward or shift
- Your IP address (the rate limiter uses it briefly and does not
  store it)

The reference code is the only link between you and your
suggestion. Keep it private if you do not want anyone else to be
able to look it up.
